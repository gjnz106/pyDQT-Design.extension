Auto Coping

by Dang Quoc Truong (DQT)

Steel framing

Modify Toolset›Auto Coping

What it does

Batch apply or remove coping on steel beams - like Revit's own Apply Coping command, but for many beam/cutter pairs at once instead of one pair at a time.

Modes

Apply coping
pick the element(s) to be CUT (coped), then the CUTTING element(s)
Remove coping
pick any related elements - coping is removed in BOTH directions, whether the picked element is the cut beam or the cutter, so there's no need to guess which side holds the coping

Workflow

  1. Run the tool and choose Apply coping or Remove coping.
  2. Apply: pick the beam(s) to be coped, then pick the cutting element(s).
  3. Remove: pick any beams involved in a coping relationship - the tool figures out and removes the coping regardless of which side you picked.
