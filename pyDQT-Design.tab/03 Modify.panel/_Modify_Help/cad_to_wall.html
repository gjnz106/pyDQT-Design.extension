CAD to Wall

by Dang Quoc Truong (DQT)

CAD conversion

Modify Toolset›CAD to Wall

What it does

Reads lines from a CAD import/link, detects parallel line pairs, computes their centerlines, and auto-creates Wall Types matching the detected thickness - turning double-line CAD wall drawings into real Revit walls.

Workflow

  1. Refresh Layers lists the CAD file's layers to choose from.
  2. Tick the layers containing wall lines.
  3. Preview shows the walls that would be created, without creating them.
  4. Pick a Level and enter a wall height.
  5. Create Walls builds the walls, auto-creating any wall type whose thickness doesn't already exist.
