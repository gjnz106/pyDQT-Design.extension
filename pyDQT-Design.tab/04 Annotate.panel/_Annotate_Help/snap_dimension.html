Snap to Grid

by Dang Quoc Truong (DQT)

Snap & align

Annotate Toolset›Snap to Grid

What it does

Nudges walls, columns, and/or beams so their dimensioned distance to the nearest grid becomes a clean round number, within a tolerance you set - cleans up "2425.042 mm" style offsets left over from import/coordination work.

Options

Scope
Entire Project or Current Selection
Category
All (Walls+Columns+Beams), or any single/pair combination
Precision
the rounding increment (e.g. round to 5 mm)
Max distance
caps how far an element may move to snap

Workflow

  1. Set Scope, Category, Precision, and Max distance.
  2. Scan lists every element with its current and proposed snapped position; tick the ones to include, or use Select All.
  3. Highlight Selected selects the ticked elements in Revit to verify them before committing.
  4. Apply Snap moves the ticked elements; 'Draw rounded line' optionally draws a detail line at the new snapped position.
